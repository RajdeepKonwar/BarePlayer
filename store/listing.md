## Full description (max 4000)

BarePlayer is a fast, clean music player for the songs already on your phone. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no internet access at all. Your music and your listening habits stay on your device.

YOUR LIBRARY, ORGANIZED
• Browse by songs, albums, artists, genres and folders
• Fast search across titles, artists and albums, even when you skip accents
• Album art from your files, including cover.jpg and folder.jpg images
• Fast scrolling through libraries with thousands of songs
• Hide folders you don't want in your library, such as voice notes or ringtones

PLAYBACK DONE RIGHT
• Now Playing screen with seek bar, shuffle and repeat
• A queue you can reorder with drag and drop, and swipe to remove
• Play next and add to queue from any song
• Keeps playing in the background with notification and lock screen controls
• Works with Bluetooth and wired headset buttons
• Pauses for calls and when headphones are unplugged
• Picks up right where you left off, even after a restart

PLAYLISTS
• Create, rename and delete playlists
• Add songs from anywhere, reorder them, and remove them

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Material You design with light, dark and system themes
• Dynamic colors that match your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer
• Plays MP3, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG, Opus, WAV and more

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
BarePlayer does not request internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no ads, no trackers, no crash reporters and no sign-in. It only reads the audio files on your device to build your library.

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL TIPS
Every feature is free. If you enjoy BarePlayer, you can leave a one-time tip from Settings to support its development. Tips are handled by Google Play and unlock nothing.
