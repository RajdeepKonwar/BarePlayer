BarePlayer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Developer: Rajdeep Konwar

BarePlayer is an offline music player for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: BarePlayer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

No internet access

BarePlayer does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it has no accounts or sign-in. Optional tips are handled by the Google Play Store app on your device (see below).

Information the app uses on your device

  • Audio files and their details (such as title, artist, album, genre, duration, folder location and album art). The app reads these from your device so it can show and play your music library.
  • Information you create in the app: playlists, your playback queue and position, play counts, excluded folders, and settings such as the theme.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Permissions

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or storage access on Android 12 and older): to find and play the audio files on your device.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls. This is optional.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep music playing when the screen is off or you switch apps.

Optional tips

BarePlayer offers optional tips through Google Play's in-app purchase system. Tips are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; BarePlayer never sees your payment details. The developer receives the standard sales reports Google Play provides to all developers (such as order numbers, amounts and country). The app only remembers on your device that you have tipped, to show a thank-you message. Tips are never required and unlock nothing.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's system backup, Android may include BarePlayer's settings and playlists in your device backup, which is handled by Android and your Google account under Google's privacy policy. BarePlayer itself never transmits this data. The music library index is excluded from backup and is rebuilt from your device.

Children

BarePlayer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
